# Sabha Parva contents (PDF pp. 21-24)

Source: Mahabharata Volume 1.pdf, PDF pages 21-24 (table of contents only). English rendering of the Hindi list. `[UNSURE: ...]` marks anything not settled from these pages.

## Sabhakriya Parva
1. At Krishna's command Maya prepares to build the assembly hall
2. Krishna's journey to Dvaraka
3. Maya brings a mace and conch for Bhimasena and Arjuna, and builds the wondrous hall
4. Dharmaraja Yudhishthira enters the hall Maya built; description of the maharshis, kings and others in it

## Lokapalasabhakhyana Parva
5. Narada comes to Yudhishthira's hall and teaches him in the form of questions
6. Yudhishthira asks about divine halls
7. Description of Indra's hall
8. Description of Yamaraja's hall
9. Description of Varuna's hall
10. Description of Kubera's hall
11. Description of Brahma's hall
12. Greatness of King Harishchandra and King Pandu's message to Yudhishthira

## Rajasuyarambha Parva
13. Yudhishthira's resolve for the Rajasuya; he consults his brothers, ministers, munis and Krishna
14. Krishna's agreement to the Rajasuya
15. Yudhishthira, Bhima and Krishna talk about Jarasandha
16. Yudhishthira loses heart about conquering Jarasandha; Arjuna's spirited words
17. Krishna supports Arjuna and tells Yudhishthira of Jarasandha's origin
18. The rakshasi Jara introduces herself and the child is named after her
19. Chandakaushika Muni foretells Jarasandha's future; his father crowns him and goes to the forest

## Jarasandhavadha Parva
20. With Yudhishthira's approval Krishna, Arjuna and Bhimasena travel to Magadha
21. Krishna praises Magadha's capital; the three break the Chaityaka peak and the drums and enter the city and palace; Krishna and Jarasandha talk
22. Jarasandha and Krishna talk; Jarasandha prepares for war; the reason for his enmity with Krishna
23. Jarasandha decides to fight Bhimasena; their terrible fight and Jarasandha's exhaustion
24. Bhima kills Jarasandha; the captive kings are freed; Krishna and others come to Indraprastha with gifts, and Krishna goes on to Dvaraka

## Digvijaya Parva
25. Arjuna and the other three brothers set out to conquer the regions
26. Arjuna defeats many lands, kings and Bhagadatta
27. Arjuna wins many mountain lands
28. After winning Kimpurusha, Hataka and Uttarakuru, Arjuna returns to Indraprastha
29. Bhimasena sets out to win the east and conquers various lands
30. Bhima wins many eastern lands and kings and returns to Indraprastha with great wealth
31. Sahadeva wins the south
32. Nakula wins the west

## Rajasuya Parva
33. Features of Yudhishthira's rule; at Krishna's command Yudhishthira takes diksha for the Rajasuya and sends invitations to kings, Brahmins and relatives
34. Kings of all lands, Kauravas and Yadavas come to the sacrifice; arrangements for food and rest
35. Description of the Rajasuya sacrifice

## Arghabhiharana Parva
36. Brahmins and kings gather at the Rajasuya; Narada describes Krishna's greatness; with Bhishma's consent Krishna receives first worship
37. Shishupala's objecting words
38. Yudhishthira reasons with Shishupala; Bhishma answers his objections
39. Sahadeva challenges the kings; the angry Shishupala and other kings prepare for battle

## Shishupalavadha Parva
40. Yudhishthira's worry and Bhishma's reassurance
41. Shishupala abuses Bhishma
42. Bhimasena's anger at Shishupala's words; Bhishma calms him
43. Bhishma tells the story of Shishupala's birth
44. Irritated by Bhishma's words, Shishupala rebukes him; Bhishma challenges all the kings to fight Krishna
45. Krishna kills Shishupala; the Rajasuya ends; all Brahmins, kings and Krishna go home

## Dyuta Parva
46. Vyasa's prophecy worries Yudhishthira; he vows to treat all equally
47. Duryodhana sees Maya's hall and becomes a laughingstock through confusion at each step; he worries at Yudhishthira's wealth
48. Shakuni and Duryodhana talk about defeating the Pandavas
49. Asked by Dhritarashtra, Duryodhana tells his worry and requests a dice game; Dhritarashtra orders Vidura to Indraprastha
50. Duryodhana tells Dhritarashtra the cause of his grief and worry
51. Duryodhana describes the gifts Yudhishthira received
52. Duryodhana describes the gifts Yudhishthira received `[UNSURE: same title as 51 in the contents; not a typing slip of mine. The chapters may differ]`
53. Duryodhana describes Yudhishthira's coronation
54. Dhritarashtra counsels Duryodhana
55. Duryodhana incites Dhritarashtra
56. Dhritarashtra and Duryodhana talk; a hall is built for the dice game; Dhritarashtra orders Vidura to summon Yudhishthira
57. Vidura and Dhritarashtra talk
58. Vidura and Yudhishthira talk; Yudhishthira goes to Hastinapura and meets everyone
59. Yudhishthira and Shakuni debate the impropriety of gambling
60. The dice game begins
61. Through Shakuni's trickery Yudhishthira loses every stake
62. Vidura warns Dhritarashtra
63. Vidura strongly opposes the gambling
64. Duryodhana rebukes Vidura and Vidura warns him
65. Yudhishthira loses wealth, kingdom, brothers, Draupadi and himself
66. Vidura rebukes Duryodhana
67. When Draupadi will not come at Pratikami's call, Duhshasana drags her to the hall by the hair; Draupadi questions the assembly
68. Bhimasena's anger and Arjuna calming him; Vikarna's righteous words opposed by Karna; Draupadi's disrobing and the Lord protecting her honour; Vidura cites Prahlada to urge the assembly to object
69. Draupadi's lament with warning, and Bhishma's words
70. Duryodhana's deceitful words and Bhimasena's furious outburst
71. Words of Karna and Duryodhana, Bhimasena's vow, Vidura's warning, and Draupadi's boons from Dhritarashtra
72. Yudhishthira calms Bhima, who is ready to kill the enemies
73. Dhritarashtra returns all of Yudhishthira's wealth, counsels him and orders him to Indraprastha

## Anudyuta Parva
74. Duryodhana tells Dhritarashtra of Arjuna's valour and asks to recall the Pandavas for another game; Dhritarashtra agrees
75. Gandhari warns Dhritarashtra and he refuses to listen
76. Despite everyone's objections, at Dhritarashtra's order Yudhishthira plays again and loses
77. Duhshasana mocks the Pandavas; Bhima, Arjuna, Nakula and Sahadeva make terrible vows to kill their enemies
78. Yudhishthira takes leave of Dhritarashtra and others; Vidura offers to keep Kunti at his home and advises the Pandavas to live by dharma
79. Draupadi takes leave of Kunti; Kunti laments and the townsmen and women grieve
80. Dhritarashtra and Vidura talk about the Pandavas' conduct on leaving for the forest and the people's grief; Drona reassures the Kauravas who come for refuge
81. Dhritarashtra's worry and his talk with Sanjaya

*(End of Sabha Parva)*
