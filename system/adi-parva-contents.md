# Adi Parva (PDF pp. 7-21)

Source: Mahabharata Volume 1.pdf, PDF pages 7-21 (table of contents only). English rendering of the Hindi list. `[UNSURE: ...]` marks anything not settled from these pages.

## Anukramanika Parva
1. Opening of the work, brief list of most topics in it, and the glory of reading it

## Parvasangraha Parva
2. Description of the Samantapanchaka region, the size of an akshauhini army, summary of the parvas and their topics, and the fruit of hearing and reading the Mahabharata

## Paushya Parva
3. Sarama's curse on Janamejaya; Janamejaya appoints Somashrava as priest; the devotion to the guru of Aruni, Upamanyu, Veda and Uttanka; Uttanka urges Janamejaya to hold the snake sacrifice

10. Dialogue of Ruru Muni and Dundubha
11. Dundubha's own story and his teaching of non-violence to Ruru
12. Ruru's curiosity about Janamejaya's snake sacrifice, answered by his father

45. Jaratkaru sees his ancestors and talks with them

51. Beginning of Janamejaya's snake sacrifice

## Flags to check

- Adi 5 and 6: Puloma is "danava" in one, "rakshasa" in the other.
- Adi 126: "cremation of the bones" after the pyre in 124.
- Adi 151-154: Hidimba (brother) and Hidimba (sister) are spelled the same in English. Hindi हिडिम्ब and हिडिम्बा.
- Repeats in the contents, as printed: Jaratkaru's marriage appears at Adi 13-14 and 45-47; Parikshit's curse and death at Adi 40-43 and 49-50.
- Names are romanised without diacritics. Verify spellings against the chapters as they are read.
